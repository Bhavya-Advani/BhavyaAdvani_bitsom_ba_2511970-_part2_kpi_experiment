


The Chi-Square test indicates a statistically significant difference in the Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate between the Control and Treatment groups (χ² = 10.6535, p = 0.001099). Since the p-value is well below the significance level of 0.05, the null hypothesis is rejected.

The Treatment group achieved a conversion rate of **7.04%**, compared with **3.19%** for the Control group, representing an absolute improvement of **3.85 percentage points** (approximately **121%** relative improvement). These results provide strong statistical evidence that the new onboarding campaign increased the likelihood of users converting from a free trial to a paid subscription.

However, the experiment also revealed important trade-offs. Although conversion improved substantially, the Treatment group experienced a higher support ticket rate and a decline in average revenue per converted user. These findings suggest that while the new onboarding campaign successfully increased conversions, additional investigation is required to determine whether the increase was accompanied by greater customer support needs or changes in the value of newly acquired subscribers.

From a business perspective, the treatment demonstrates strong potential but should be evaluated alongside the guardrail metrics before a full-scale rollout is recommended.
